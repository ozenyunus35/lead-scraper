Business leads, scraped and cleaned automatically

This scraper crawls a business directory page by page, opens every listing, normalises phone numbers, merges duplicate businesses and exports a clean CSV and Excel file. It runs again every day on its own, and this page shows the latest result.

What happens on every run

  1. Crawl politely. Checks robots.txt, waits between requests, retries with back-off, identifies itself with a user agent.
  2. Follow the structure. Category pages → pagination → each business page, collecting every listing once.
  3. Clean. Phone numbers in any format become one standard (E.164); addresses split into street, city, state, ZIP.
  4. De-duplicate. The same business listed twice, even under a slightly different name, is merged by phone number, keeping the most complete record and all its categories.
  5. Export. CSV, a formatted Excel workbook and JSON, published here by a scheduled GitHub Actions job.

The business directory is a demo site with invented businesses (phone numbers in the fictional 555-01xx range), built for this showcase. The books dataset comes from books.toscrape.com, a public sandbox made for scraping practice.
